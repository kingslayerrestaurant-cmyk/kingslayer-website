Our Story

Kingslayer Ballia —
Where Hospitality Reigns

Kingslayer Ballia was founded with a singular vision: to offer the people of Ballia and the surrounding region a dining experience befitting a king. We set out to create a space where the finest flavours of Indian and international cuisine meet an atmosphere of refined elegance.

From our premium restaurant floor to our expansive banquet facilities and our celebrated rooftop — every corner of Kingslayer has been designed with one purpose: to make every occasion extraordinary.

Premium Dining

A curated menu of fine Indian cuisine with international influences, crafted by our experienced kitchen team.

Rooftop Ambience

Dine above the cityscape. Our rooftop offers a unique open-air experience unlike anything in Ballia.

Banquet Excellence

From intimate celebrations to grand weddings — Kingslayer's banquet facilities are equipped for every occasion.

Warm Hospitality

A trained, attentive team committed to ensuring every guest feels genuinely welcomed and cared for.
