Culinary Artistry

Flavours Crafted with
Royal Reverence

Our kitchen marries time-honoured slow-cooking traditions with contemporary presentation. Every marinade is blended fresh, every spice roasted to release its purest aromatic notes.

From the velvety richness of our slow-simmered Dal Makhani to tandoori kebabs kissed by charcoal smoke, our dining room celebrates North Indian gastronomy in its most elevated expression.

✦ Overnight slow-braised lentils and heritage Awadhi gravies
✦ Authentic clay tandoor grills and freshly baked artisanal breads
✦ Strict quality sourcing with zero artificial additives
