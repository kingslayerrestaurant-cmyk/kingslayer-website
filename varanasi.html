Our Story

Kingslayer Varanasi —
Heritage Elevated

Varanasi — the city of Shiva, of ghats, of ancient rhythms and timeless stories. Kingslayer Varanasi was established to offer this extraordinary city a dining experience worthy of its cultural stature. A space where the depth of Indian culinary heritage meets contemporary luxury.

From our elegant main dining floor to our rooftop that captures the spirit of the city, and our grand banquet facilities — every detail at Kingslayer Varanasi has been considered with the highest standards of hospitality in mind.

Cultural Dining

A menu rooted in the rich culinary traditions of Varanasi and the broader Awadhi heritage, elevated with contemporary refinement.

Rooftop Setting

Dine with the energy of Varanasi below. An open-air experience that captures the essence of this magnificent city.

Grand Banquets

From elaborate weddings to corporate affairs — Kingslayer Varanasi's banquet facilities are designed for grandeur.

Genuine Warmth

Hospitality is the soul of Varanasi. Our team reflects that deep tradition of warmth and genuine care in everything we do.
